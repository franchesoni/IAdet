How to use:

  • Add bounding box: Two left clicks to define corners
  • Remove bounding box: Right-click near a box center (removes the closest one)
  • Pan: Middle-click drag, or Ctrl+drag, or Shift+drag
  • Zoom: Mouse wheel
  • Navigate: Use Previous/Next buttons or arrow keys
  • Clear all: Remove all bounding boxes
  • Reset view: Fit image to canvas

Note: Annotations are automatically saved when you navigate to another image.
